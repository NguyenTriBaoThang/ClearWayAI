## 1. Core Principle of Safe Operation

ClearWayAI operates on a zero-hazard principle: **no artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning agent, or remote telemetry feed is ever granted unconstrained direct write access to physical traffic signals.**

All commands generated by the ClearWayAI coordination engine pass through an independent, deterministic **Signal Safety Gateway (SSG)** emulating a hardware **Malfunction Management Unit (MMU / Conflict Monitor Unit CMU)** before being accepted into the simulation or pilot signal controller.


## 2. Formal Safety Invariants

### Invariant 1 (INV-01): Conflict-Free Vehicular Mutual Exclusion
- **Statement:** Opposing or intersecting vehicular movements shall never display concurrent Green or Yellow signal indications.
- **Formal Expression:**
  $$\forall t, \quad \left( \Phi_{\text{EW}}(t) \in \{\text{GREEN}, \text{YELLOW}\} \right) \implies \left( \Phi_{\text{NS}}(t) = \text{RED} \right)$$
  $$\forall t, \quad \left( \Phi_{\text{NS}}(t) \in \{\text{GREEN}, \text{YELLOW}\} \right) \implies \left( \Phi_{\text{EW}}(t) = \text{RED} \right)$$
- **Enforcement:** If a proposed state violates INV-01, the safety gateway trips immediately to **Fail-Safe All-Red** ($t_{\text{flash}} = 5.0\text{ s}$).

### Invariant 2 (INV-02): Minimum Green Interval Enforcement
- **Statement:** Once a vehicular approach transitions to Green, it shall remain Green for at least $T_{\text{min\_green}}$ seconds to avoid driver indecision zone dilemmas and rear-end collisions.
- **Threshold:**
  $$T_{\text{min\_green}} \ge 10.0\text{ seconds}$$
- **Pre-emption Override Policy:** Even during high-priority emergency pre-clearance requests (AMB-339), an existing cross-street green phase cannot be truncated until $T_{\text{min\_green}}$ has fully elapsed.

### Invariant 3 (INV-03): Maximum Green Interval Enforcement
- **Statement:** An approach shall not hold Green indefinitely, regardless of queued volume or extended priority requests, preventing starvation of cross streets and pedestrian crossings.
- **Threshold:**
  $$T_{\text{max\_green}} \le 75.0\text{ seconds}$$

### Invariant 4 (INV-04): Minimum Yellow Change Interval
- **Statement:** Every vehicular green phase must terminate with a mandatory Yellow Change interval derived from the ITE kinematic equation:
  $$Y = t_{\text{reaction}} + \frac{v}{2(a + gG)}$$
- **Threshold:**
  $$T_{\text{yellow}} \ge 3.0\text{ seconds (Standard: 4.0s)}$$
- **Enforcement:** Yellow intervals can never be truncated, skipped, or reduced to zero under any emergency condition.

### Invariant 5 (INV-05): Minimum All-Red Clearance Interval
- **Statement:** Following each Yellow change interval, all signal heads shall display solid Red for an All-Red Clearance interval to ensure the intersection conflict box is completely vacated before conflicting traffic receives Green:
  $$R_{\text{clearance}} = \frac{W + L}{v}$$
- **Threshold:**
  $$T_{\text{all\_red}} \ge 2.0\text{ seconds}$$

### Invariant 6 (INV-06): Communication Loss & Heartbeat Timeout Fallback
- **Statement:** If edge controllers lose V2X/RSU communication or backend telemetry heartbeats for more than $T_{\text{timeout}}$, controllers must autonomously degrade to local actuated or fixed-time coordination.
- **Threshold:**
  $$T_{\text{heartbeat\_timeout}} = 500\text{ ms}$$
- **Fallback Action:** Log warning `V2X_SEVERED_FALLBACK_LOCAL`, alert the operations dashboard, and maintain localized loop detector timing.

## 3. Pedestrian Safety Boundaries

1. **Pedestrian Walk Minimum:**
   $$T_{\text{walk}} \ge 7.0\text{ seconds}$$
2. **Pedestrian Clearance (Flashing Don't Walk - FDW):**
   Calculated based on a conservative walking speed of $1.0\text{ m/s}$ across the $14.0\text{ m}$ roadway cross-section:
   $$T_{\text{FDW}} \ge \frac{14.0\text{ m}}{1.0\text{ m/s}} = 14.0\text{ seconds (Standard: 15.0–18.0s)}$$
3. **No Direct Turn Collisions:**
   Pedestrian WALK signals shall never be active concurrently with permissive vehicular left turns across the crosswalk during emergency corridor pre-emption.

## 4. Operational Modes & Public Infrastructure Safeguards

```
              ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
              │          ClearWayAI System           │
              │  Default Mode: SIMULATION            │
              │  public_signal_control = FALSE       │
              └──────────────────┬───────────────────┘
                                 │
            ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
            ▼                    ▼                    ▼
     [ SIMULATION ]      [ SHADOW ADVISORY ]  [ AUTHORIZED PILOT ]
     - Pure in-silico    - Read-only sensors  - Multi-sig keys required
     - Zero real cables  - Zero actuation     - Hardwired MMU tripwire
     - DEMO_FIXTURE      - SHADOW_ADVISORY    - Authorized personnel
```

1. **Physical Actuator Air-Gap:**
   In development, staging, and demo environments, the hardware bridge is logically and physically disconnected.
2. **Provenance Watermark:**
   All data emitted by the system carries a mandatory `data_provenance` tag. Telemetry without a validated cryptographic signature cannot be promoted above `DEMO_FIXTURE`.
3. **Fail-Safe Flash Default:**
   In the event of hardware power loss or CPU lockup, the controller default relay state drops to physical flashing amber/red.
